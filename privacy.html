Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

Soonish is made by Small Wins Studio LLC, which is responsible for this policy and for the app.

The short version: Soonish doesn't collect any data. Everything you put in the app stays on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud Drive if you keep its daily backup on.

What we collect

Nothing. Soonish has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. It never sends your tasks, notes, focus time or progress to us or anyone else. The only times it reaches beyond your phone are through Apple: the backup to your own iCloud Drive, and the App Store when you buy or restore Soonish Plus.

Where your data lives

Your tasks and everything else you add are stored on your iPhone, and shared only with Soonish's own widgets on the same device. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple includes Soonish's data in your device backup like any other app's. That backup is managed by Apple, and we can't see it.

Soonish also keeps its own backup: once a day it saves a copy of your list (tasks, history, badges and settings) to a Soonish folder in your own iCloud Drive, so you can bring everything back if you reinstall the app. It's stored by Apple in your iCloud account, and we can't see it. You can turn it off, or save a copy anywhere you choose, in Soonish → Settings → Your list.

Permissions the app may ask for

Each one is optional, and you can turn it off at any time in the iPhone's Settings app.

  • Calendar: to show today's events next to your picks for the day. Soonish only reads your calendar; it never changes it, and the events never leave your phone.
  • Reminders: to show the reminders due today from the lists you choose, and to mark one done when you tick it in Soonish. Soonish changes nothing else, and your reminders never leave your phone.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: to turn what you say into tasks. Speech is recognized on your iPhone, and the audio isn't saved or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications: for the gentle nudges and the end-of-day note. They're scheduled on your phone.
  • Camera: to take a photo of a written list (Snap a list). The text is read on your iPhone, and the photo isn't kept.
  • Photos: when you choose a photo yourself, to attach to a task, read a list from, or use as the background of the Your photo theme. Soonish only gets the photos you pick, and keeps them on your iPhone.
  • Face ID: to keep your kept thoughts private. Apple handles the check; Soonish never sees your face data.

Files you attach

Files and photos you attach to a task are stored on your iPhone, inside Soonish. Deleting the task deletes them.

Apple Intelligence

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Break it down suggests small first steps using Apple's on-device model. Your task stays on your iPhone; nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you add tasks with Siri, Apple handles the voice request under Apple's privacy policy. Soonish only receives the task text and saves it on your phone.

Soonish Plus purchases

Soonish Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and your account details; Soonish only learns whether Plus is active on your Apple Account, and checks it on your phone. To cancel, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Deleting your data

Because everything lives on your phone, you're in control of it:

  • Delete a task, a kept thought or an attachment in the app, and it's gone from your phone.
  • Delete the Soonish app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone.
  • To remove the backup, turn it off in Soonish → Settings → Your list, then delete the Soonish folder in the Files app → iCloud Drive.

We don't hold a copy of anything, so there's nothing for us to delete. If you ever emailed us, ask and we'll delete those emails.

Feedback emails

If you choose to send feedback from the app, your email includes the app version and iOS version so we can help. We use your email only to reply, and we never share it.

Children

Soonish doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example if a future version adds optional sync, we'll update this page and the date above before the change ships.

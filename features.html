What Soonish can do.

It starts with one thing a day. Everything below is there when you want it, and tucked away when you don’t.

Adding

Get it out of your head.

Type it the way you’d say it. Soonish reads the day, the repeat and the rest, and files it for you.

  • One line at a time. Press return and keep going. Each line is a task.
  • Paste a whole list. From Notes, a message or an email, every line becomes its own task.
  • Say it. Tap the mic and talk: “call the dentist, renew my registration, email Sam.” You see the tasks before they’re saved.
  • Snap a list. Take a photo of a sticky note, a whiteboard or a paper list. Each line is read on your iPhone, and the photo isn’t kept.
  • A list from a file. Text, Markdown, CSV, rich text or PDF. Items already checked off are left out.
  • Doubles, spotted. Pasted a list twice? Keep both, or remove the extra in one tap.
  • From anywhere. Siri, Control Center, the Lock Screen, or a touch and hold on the app icon.

Dates without planning ahead.

call mom tomorrow rent due the 30th gym every mon wed fri until nov 30 advisor call at 6pm
  • Just type the day. Write it the way you’d say it. Soonish reads the day and time, and the title stays clean.
  • Repeats in plain words. Every Sunday, every weekday, every Mon and Thu, with an end date when you want one.
  • Times and heads-ups. A time sets itself, and an optional heads-up reminds you before it.
  • Plans and deadlines. “On Friday” waits for Friday. “By Friday” can be picked earlier, from a week before.
  • See the day first. Picking a date shows what’s already in Soonish that day, and your calendar too.

Picking

Stop deciding what to do first.

  • One thing a day. Picked for you each morning, with a short reason like “Due today” or “Quick win.”
  • Grow when you’re ready. Once one a day is a habit, Soonish asks if you’d like two, then three. Never more, and you can always go back.
  • Important comes first. Mark what matters, and it goes first, right after anything due today.
  • Light, medium or heavy. At most one heavy one a day, unless more are due. When two things are due together, you start with the lighter one.
  • Swap it, or not today. Don’t like the pick? Trade it, push it to tomorrow, or delete it. Undo is one tap away.
  • Keep going. Done with your one? Get one more, as many times as you like.

Starting

Starting is the hardest part.

  • Start for 5 minutes. One tap starts a short timer on your one thing. Stopping early still counts.
  • Break it down.Plus Three tiny first steps for a task that feels too big, on iPhone models with Apple Intelligence. Today shows one step at a time to check off.
  • Focus mode. 5, 15 or 25 minutes, with a sun crossing a small arc as the time passes, on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island too.
  • Gentle nudges. A morning start and a midday check, only about what’s left. They stop the moment you’re done.
  • An evening note. Everything you got done today. On a quiet day, it stays quiet.

Wins

Feel every win.

  • The sun rises. Check off your one thing and a little light flies up into the sun. Finish from a widget or Siri, and it rises when you next open Today.
  • Steady and Perfect weeks. Something done every day you’re on earns a Steady week. Days off are skipped, never held against you.
  • Badges you keep. Metal pins that catch the light as you tilt your phone, for milestones, focus hours, fresh starts and more, plus a few hidden ones for the curious. Share any of them as a picture.
  • Your accomplishments. Every badge is something you did, kept in a sky of constellations. Open one any time to see it again.
  • Wins only go up. Everything you’ve done since you started, your last six weeks, and a month of little suns.
  • The Done tab. Today from morning to evening, then every day before it, with search, notes, links and files.
  • Your patterns.Plus After about a week, Soonish notices how you work: when you finish the most, your strongest day, how you come back after a day off. Tap one to see why.

Hard days

When it’s all too much.

  • Need a minute. One tap turns the day to night and hides everything else: three slow breaths with the moon over a lake, a 2-minute reset, or a private place to clear your head.
  • Take a day off. A beach opens, you pick the day, and a wave washes in. No nudges, and it never breaks a streak. One day or a whole vacation.
  • Forgot yesterday? If your week’s badge is on the line, Soonish offers to count yesterday as a day off. Only yesterday.
  • Quiet days count too. A day with nothing on your list is simply quiet. Your streak is safe.
  • No red number unless you ask for one. No “overdue,” and no “you missed.”

Everywhere

Out of sight, but not out of mind.

  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Check off your one thing under the same sky and sun, add a task, or start Need a minute, one tap away.
  • Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.Plus Today’s picks live right there, and you can check them off without opening the app.
  • Control Center and the Action button. Add a task, open Today, take a day off or start Need a minute.
  • Siri and Shortcuts. “What’s my one thing?”, mark it done or take today off, hands-free.
  • Your calendar on Today. Your events and Apple Reminders sit on the sun’s path, beside your one thing. Both optional.
  • Light and dark. Soonish follows your iPhone, or you choose. At night, Today’s sky turns deep blue with faint stars.

Apple Intelligence

A little help, on your iPhone.

  • Smarter picks.Free Soonish gets a better sense of each task, quick or big, and whether it matters even with no deadline, like renewing a passport.
  • Untangle what you say.Free Say it all in one go, rambling and all, and Soonish sorts it into separate tasks, dates included.
  • Break it down.Plus Tiny first steps for a task that feels too big, checked off one at a time on Today.
  • Right on your iPhone. These work on iPhone 15 Pro or newer and recent iPad models, with Apple Intelligence turned on. Everything stays on your device. And if you’ve set something yourself, like marking a task Important, Soonish goes with your choice.

Your data

Your data stays yours.

  • No account. No sign-in, no ads and no tracking. Soonish doesn’t collect anything.
  • Backed up to your iCloud Drive. Once a day, so your list comes back if you reinstall. Or save a copy anywhere you like.
  • Private thoughts stay private. What you keep from Need a minute opens with Face ID.

Plus

Soonish Plus.

Soonish is free, with no limits. Plus is extra, for people who want a little more.

  • Themes and app icons. Twelve more themes: calm colors, clean ones like Ink and Pearl, brushed metals, and bold ones like Aurora or your own photo. Each has its own sky, or just its color if you turn off sky details.
  • Break it down. Tiny first steps for a big task, on iPhone models with Apple Intelligence.
  • Your patterns. When you finish the most, your strongest day, your longest run, and gentle suggestions that fit how you work. Nothing changes unless you say yes.
  • Today on the Lock Screen. Check off your picks right there, and see them in the Dynamic Island and on your Apple Watch.
  • StandBy. Your day’s sun and what’s next, while your iPhone charges on its side.
  • Focus sounds. Soft brown, pink or white noise under any timer, even with your phone locked.
  • Add from your Mac. Add to a Soonish list in Reminders on your Mac, iPad or Apple Watch, and it moves into your Scratchpad with its day, time, notes and link. Needs iCloud with Reminders on.

In the app: no account, and nothing is ever sent to us. Free, with no limits.

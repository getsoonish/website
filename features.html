What Soonish can do

Made by someone with ADHD, for minds that freeze at a long list. It starts with one thing a day. Everything below is there when you want it, and tucked away when you don't.

Get it out of your head

  • Type one line at a time. Press return and keep going. Each line is a task.
  • Paste a whole list from Notes, a message or an email, and every line becomes its own task.
  • Say it. Tap the mic and talk: "call the dentist, renew my registration, email Sam". You see the tasks before they're saved.
  • Snap a list. Take a photo of a sticky note, a whiteboard or a paper list. Each line is read on your iPhone, and anything already on your list is left out. The photo isn't kept.
  • Ask Siri. "Add to Soonish: pay the water bill."
  • From anywhere: Control Center and touching and holding the app icon both take you straight to adding.

Stop deciding what to do first

  • One thing a day, picked for you each morning, with a short reason like "Due today" or "Quick win".
  • Grow when you're ready. After a Steady week, Soonish asks if you'd like two a day, then three. Never more, and you can always go back.
  • Mark what matters Important, and it comes first.
  • Tell it how big a task is, if you like: light, medium or heavy. It never gives you two heavy ones on the same day. When two things are due together, it starts you with the lighter one, and when it's all too much, Need a minute shows the easy ones first.
  • Swap, Not today, Delete. Don't like the pick? Swap it, push it to tomorrow, or delete it. Undo is always one tap away.
  • Keep going. Done with your one? Get one more, as many times as you like. With today cleared, it looks ahead: tomorrow's first, then the rest of the week.

Starting is the hardest part

  • Start for 5 minutes. One tap starts a short focus timer on your first task. Stopping early still counts.
  • Focus mode for 5, 15 or 25 minutes, with a timer on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Gentle nudges. A morning start and a midday check, only about what's left. They stop the moment you're done.
  • An end-of-day note about everything you got done. Nothing on a day you didn't.

Feel every win

  • You're celebrated every time you check something off, and a little more when your goal is done. With Reduce Motion on, the celebration stays still.
  • Steady and Perfect weeks. Something done every day you're on earns a Steady week. Days off are skipped, not held against you.
  • Badges you keep: milestones, focus hours, Above and beyond, Self-starter, Welcome back, and seasonal ones like Fresh start in the first week of January. Share any of them as a picture.
  • Progress that only goes up: everything you've done since you started, a bar for each week, and a month of dots.
  • The Done tab keeps everything you finished, by day, with search, notes, links and files.

Dates without planning ahead

  • Just type the day. "call mom tomorrow", "dentist fri", "rent due the 30th". The day comes out of the title.
  • Repeats in plain words. "water plants every sunday", "gym every mon wed fri", "standup every weekday", with an end if you like: "until Nov 30".
  • Times and heads-ups. "advisor call at 6pm" sets a time, and an optional heads-up reminds you before it.
  • Plans and deadlines are different. "On Friday" waits for Friday. "By Friday" can be picked earlier, from a week before.
  • See your day before you choose it. Picking a date shows what's already in Soonish that day, and your calendar too.

When it's all too much

  • Need a minute. One tap hides everything. Choose a 2-minute reset, one small timed step, or a private place to clear your head, then a breathing sunrise when you're ready to go back.
  • Take a day off. No nudges, no note, and it never breaks a streak. Plan a single day or a whole vacation.
  • Forgot to check something off? If your week's badge is on the line, Soonish asks that evening if you're taking a day off, with a button to take it. It's one notification at your end-of-day time, and you can turn it off in Settings. And the next morning it offers to count yesterday as a day off, once. Only yesterday: more than a day back can't be changed.
  • Quiet days count too. A day with nothing on your list is simply quiet. Your streak is safe.
  • Never a red number, never "overdue", never "you missed". On a day with nothing done, the most you'll hear is an offer to take it off.

Out of sight, out of mind? Not here

  • Home Screen widgets you can check off right there, instead of a red number on the icon.
  • Lock Screen widgets for your progress, opening Soonish and Need a minute.
  • Control Center buttons, and the Action button on iPhones that have one.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: ask “What’s my one thing?”, mark it done, or take today off, hands-free. They’re in the Shortcuts app too, ready for your own automations, like hearing your one thing when your alarm stops.
  • Your day on Today: your calendar's events and the reminders due today from Apple's Reminders, side by side with your one thing. Both are optional.
  • Light and dark, following your iPhone or set your own way.

With Apple Intelligence

  • Untangle what you say Free Say it all in one go, rambling and all, and Soonish sorts it into separate tasks, keeping words like “friday” so the dates work.
  • Break it down Plus Three tiny first steps for a task that feels too big.
  • On iPhone 15 Pro or newer and recent iPads with Apple Intelligence turned on. It all happens on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Your data stays yours

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking. Soonish doesn't collect anything.
  • Backed up to your own iCloud Drive once a day, so if you ever reinstall, your list comes back (attached files aren't included). Or save a copy anywhere you like, such as Files or Google Drive.
  • Private thoughts stay private. What you keep from Need a minute opens with Face ID.

Soonish Plus

Soonish is free, with no limits. Plus is extra, for people who want a little more.

  • Themes and app icons: calm colors, clean ones like Ink and Pearl, and bold ones like Aurora, Holo, or your own photo.
  • Focus sounds: soft brown, pink or white noise under any timer, even with your phone locked.
  • Today on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, ready to check off right there, and on your Apple Watch.
  • StandBy: your day's ring and what's next, while your iPhone charges on its side.
  • Break it down: on iPhones with Apple Intelligence, three tiny first steps for a task that feels too big (see above).
  • Add from your Mac: add to a Soonish list in Apple's Reminders on your Mac, Apple Watch, iPad, or with Siri, and it moves into your Scratchpad the next time you open Soonish, dates and all. Once it's moved, it's checked off in Reminders, so Soonish stays your one list.
  • Your patterns: after about a week of use, when you finish the most, your best day, your longest run, and a short note on the 1st about the month before. It also suggests changes that fit how you actually work, like moving your nudges or end-of-day note to the times you really get things done. Nothing changes unless you say yes. After about six weeks, you can see how your patterns have changed since your first month.

No account. Nothing is ever sent to us. Free, with no limits. Plus is an optional extra.

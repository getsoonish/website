What Soonish can do

Made by someone with ADHD, for minds that freeze at a long list. It starts with one thing a day. Everything below is there when you want it, and tucked away when you don't.

Get it out of your head

Type it the way you'd say it. Soonish reads the day, the repeat and the rest, and files it for you.

  • Type one line at a time. Press return and keep going. Each line is a task.
  • Paste a whole list. From Notes, a message or an email, every line becomes its own task.
  • Say it. Tap the mic and talk: "call the dentist, renew my registration, email Sam". You see the tasks before they're saved.
  • Snap a list. Take a photo of a sticky note, a whiteboard or a paper list. Each line is read on your iPhone, and anything already on your list is left out. The photo isn't kept.
  • A list from a file. Choose a text, Markdown, CSV, rich text or PDF file, and each line becomes a task. Items already checked off are left out. The file isn't kept.
  • Doubles, spotted. Pasted a list twice? Soonish spots tasks that are nearly the same, so you can keep both or remove the extra in one tap.
  • Ask Siri. "Add to Soonish: pay the water bill."
  • Add from anywhere. Control Center, the Lock Screen and touching and holding the app icon all take you straight to adding.

Stop deciding what to do first

  • One thing a day. It’s picked for you each morning, with a short reason like "Due today" or "Quick win".
  • Grow when you're ready. After a Steady week, Soonish asks if you'd like two a day, then three. Never more, and you can always go back.
  • Important comes first. Mark what matters as Important, and Soonish puts it first, right after anything due today.
  • Light, medium or heavy. Tell Soonish how big a task is. It never gives you two heavy ones on the same day, and when two things are due together, it starts you with the lighter one.
  • Swap, Not today, Delete. Don't like the pick? Swap it, push it to tomorrow, or delete it. Undo is always one tap away.
  • Keep going. Done with your one? Get one more, as many times as you like.

Starting is the hardest part

  • Start for 5 minutes. One tap starts a short focus timer on your one thing. Stopping early still counts.
  • Break it down. Plus Three tiny first steps for a task that feels too big, read from the task and its note. Use them, and Today shows one step at a time to check off. Still too big? Make any step smaller.
  • Focus mode. A timer for 5, 15 or 25 minutes under a night sky, on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island too.
  • Gentle nudges. A morning start and a midday check, only about what's left. They stop the moment you're done.
  • An end-of-day note. It tells you everything you got done, and stays quiet on a day you didn't.

Feel every win

  • The sun rises. Check off your one thing and a little light flies up into the sun. Finish from a widget or Siri, and it rises the next time you open Today. With Reduce Motion on, it stays still.
  • Steady and Perfect weeks. Something done every day you're on earns a Steady week. Days off are skipped, not held against you.
  • Badges you keep. Metal pins that catch the light as you tilt your phone, for milestones, focus hours, Above and beyond, Self-starter, Welcome back, and seasonal ones like Fresh start in the first week of January. Share any of them as a picture.
  • Your accomplishments, all in one place. Every badge on your shelf is something you did, and it's yours to keep. Come back any time and open one to see it again.

Look back on what you did

  • Progress that only goes up. Everything you've done since you started, your last six weeks as a skyline, and a month of little suns.
  • The Done tab. Everything you finished, by day, with search, notes, links and files. Today runs from morning to evening.
  • Your patterns. Plus After about a week, Soonish notices how you work: when you finish the most, how often you keep going once you start, how you come back after a day off, and when your big tasks get done. A different one leads each week, with its own picture.

Dates without planning ahead

  • Just type the day. "call mom tomorrow", "dentist fri", "rent due the 30th". The day comes out of the title.
  • Repeats in plain words. "water plants every sunday", "gym every mon wed fri", "standup every weekday", and an end date when you want one: "until Nov 30".
  • Times and heads-ups. "advisor call at 6pm" sets a time, and an optional heads-up reminds you before it.
  • Plans and deadlines are different. "On Friday" waits for Friday. "By Friday" can be picked earlier, from a week before.
  • See your day before you choose it. Picking a date shows what's already in Soonish that day, and your calendar too.

When it's all too much

  • Need a minute. One tap turns the day to night and hides everything else. Choose a 2-minute reset, one small timed step, a private place to clear your head, or three slow breaths with the moon over a lake.
  • Take a day off. A beach opens, you pick the day, and a wave washes in. No nudges, no note, and it never breaks a streak. Plan a single day or a whole vacation.
  • Forgot to check something off? If your week's badge is on the line, Soonish asks that evening if you're taking a day off, and the next morning offers to count yesterday as one. Only yesterday: more than a day back can't be changed.
  • Quiet days count too. A day with nothing on your list is simply quiet. Your streak is safe.
  • Never a red number. No "overdue" and no "you missed".

Out of sight, out of mind? Not here

  • Home Screen widgets. Check off your one thing right there, under the same sky and sun, instead of seeing a red number on the icon.
  • Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Your progress, adding a task and Need a minute, one tap away. With Plus, today's picks live on your Lock Screen too.
  • Control Center and the Action button. Add a task, open Today, take a day off or start Need a minute.
  • Siri and Shortcuts. Ask “What’s my one thing?”, mark it done, or take today off, hands-free, or use them in your own automations.
  • Your calendar on Today. See your next calendar event and the reminders due today from Apple's Reminders, right alongside your one thing. Both are optional.
  • Light and dark. Soonish follows your iPhone, or you set it your own way. At night, Today's sky turns deep blue with faint stars.

With Apple Intelligence

  • Smarter picks. Free Soonish gets a better sense of each task, whether it's quick or a big one, and whether it matters even with no deadline, like renewing a passport, so the one thing it picks fits. What you set yourself always wins.
  • Untangle what you say. Free Say it all in one go, rambling and all, and Soonish sorts it into separate tasks, keeping words like “friday” so the dates work.
  • Break it down. Plus Tiny first steps for a task that feels too big, read from the task and its note, and checked off one at a time on Today.
  • On iPhone 15 Pro or newer and recent iPad models, with Apple Intelligence turned on. It all happens on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Your data stays yours

  • No account. No sign-in, no ads and no tracking. Soonish doesn't collect anything.
  • Backed up to your own iCloud Drive. Once a day, so if you ever reinstall, your list comes back (attached files aren't included). Or save a copy anywhere you like, such as Files or Google Drive.
  • Private thoughts stay private. What you keep from Need a minute opens with Face ID.

Soonish Plus

Soonish is free, with no limits. Plus is extra, for people who want a little more.

  • Themes and app icons. Calm colors, clean ones like Ink and Pearl, and bold ones like Aurora, Holo, or your own photo. Every theme gets its own sky.
  • Focus sounds. Soft brown, pink or white noise under any timer, even with your phone locked.
  • Today on the Lock Screen. Check off your picks right there, and see them in the Dynamic Island and on your Apple Watch.
  • StandBy. Your day's ring and what's next, while your iPhone charges on its side.
  • Break it down. On iPhone models with Apple Intelligence, tiny first steps for a task that feels too big (see above).
  • Add from your Mac. Add to a Soonish list in Apple's Reminders on your Mac, Apple Watch, iPad, or with Siri, and it moves into your Scratchpad the next time you open Soonish, with its date, notes and link.
  • Your patterns. After about a week of use, see when you finish the most, how often you keep going once you start, how you come back after a day off, when your big tasks get done, your best day, your longest run, and a short note on the 1st about the month before. It also suggests changes that fit how you actually work. Nothing changes unless you say yes. After about six weeks, you can see how your patterns have changed since your first month.

No account. Nothing is ever sent to us. Free, with no limits. Plus is an optional extra.

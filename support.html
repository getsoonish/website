Your day

Why only one thing a day?

Starting is the hardest part, so Soonish starts you with one. Once one a day has become a habit, it asks if you’d like two a day, then three. You can change it any time in Settings → Daily goal, and doing more is always welcome: it all counts.

A day with nothing on your list is a quiet day, and it never breaks your streak.

How does Soonish pick for me?

Anything due today comes first, then tasks you’ve marked Important, then what’s due soon and how long things have been waiting. A task set for a later day waits for that day, and a deadline more than a week away waits until it’s closer. It never gives you more than one task marked Heavy at once, unless two are due today.

Each pick shows a small reason. Don’t like one? Swap it out, and use Back to Soonish’s picks to undo.

Done with today and want more? Keep going brings the next thing: anything left for today, then what’s coming soonest, then things with no date. The Scratchpad’s Up next always shows the same thing.

I finished something that wasn’t in today’s picks. Does it count?

Yes. Anything you finish counts toward your daily goal, whether it’s one of today’s picks or something from the Scratchpad. The sun rises on Today, nudges stop, and the day’s little sun lights up. It all counts toward Wins and badges too.

I checked something off by accident.

Tap the check again to undo it. Or tap ••• (or touch and hold the task) and choose Mark as not done. You can also find it in the Done tab. It goes back exactly where it was.

Dates

How do I put a task on a certain day?

Just type the day anywhere in the line: call mom tomorrow, dentist fri, rent oct 30 or 10/30. The task waits in the Scratchpad and joins today’s picks that morning. this weekend means Saturday, and next fri means Friday of next week.

You can also tap ••• on a task and choose Set a date.

What’s the difference between a day and a deadline?

A plain date (fri) is the day you’ll do it, so it stays out of today’s picks until then. Add by or due (by fri) and it’s a deadline instead: Soonish may pick it earlier when there’s room. Switch between the two under Set a date or in Edit.

I gave a task the wrong date.

Tap ••• on the task and choose Set a date (or Change date). This works on Today too: pick a later day and it leaves today’s list and waits for that day.

Can tasks repeat?

Yes. Type every day, every weekday, every mon and thu, on tuesdays, weekly or each day this week, or tap ••• and choose Repeat to pick the days and an optional last day.

Each repeat joins today’s picks on its day. Check it off and the next one waits for its day. Miss a day and it simply moves on: nothing piles up, and nothing counts against you.

Can Soonish remind me at a certain time?

Yes. Add a time when you type a task (call doctor at 3pm, dentist fri 9:30am or just 320p), or tap •••, then Change date and Set time.

Then turn on Settings → Nudges → Heads-up for timed tasks and choose 15 minutes, 30 minutes or an hour before. You get one notification, even on a day off, and none if the task is already done.

Days off

How do I take a day off?

At the top of Today, tap the umbrella and pick the day. Days off never break your streak, and the app stays quiet on them. To skip the same days every week, change Active days in Settings.

Can I take a day off after the fact?

For yesterday, yes. If yesterday had nothing checked off and that would cost your week its Steady or Perfect week, Soonish asks the next morning if you’d like to count it as a day off. It only goes back one day.

To catch it the same day, keep Day off reminder on in Settings. If nothing’s checked off by your evening note’s time and your week is on the line, Soonish asks if you’re taking a day off, with a button to take it.

What is Need a minute?

For when it’s all too much. Tap Need a minute on Today (or use its Lock Screen widget) and everything else hides. Choose three slow breaths, a 2-minute reset, or Clear my head, where you can write or say what’s on your mind.

Let it go clears it away and saves nothing. Keep it saves it privately on your phone under Kept thoughts, which open with Face ID.

Badges

How do badges work?

You earn badges for Steady and Perfect weeks, your best week yet, going above and beyond, doing things you chose yourself, coming back after time away, focus time and milestones. There are once-a-year ones too, for New Year, St. Patrick’s Day, Halloween, Thanksgiving and each new season, and one for your first day off.

They never expire, and days off never cost you one. Find them in Wins: tap one to bring it to the front, and tap it again to see why you earned it.

Can I share a badge?

Yes. Tap Share when you earn one, or open any badge in Wins and tap Share. It makes a picture of the badge and why you earned it, never your tasks, and you choose where it goes.

Soonish Plus

Is there an iPad, Mac or Apple Watch app?

Not yet. Soonish is made for iPhone. With Soonish Plus, your one thing already shows on your Apple Watch in the Smart Stack. Apps for Apple Watch, iPad and Mac are on the way, and we’ll say so in the app’s update notes when each one is ready.

What is Soonish Plus?

A few extras on top of Soonish, which stays free. They’re free for everyone during the beta, marked with a small Plus tag. Turn them on in Settings → Soonish Plus.

  • Today on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. It shows up when you open Soonish and today’s picks aren’t all done, and you can check them off right there. The first time, iOS asks to allow Live Activities.
  • Themes and app icons. Calm colors (Sea glass, Dusk, Rosewood, Brick, Denim), clean ones (Ink, Pearl), brushed metals (Titanium, Gunmetal, Rose gold) and bold ones (Aurora and your own photo), each with a matching icon. Sunrise, the default, is free. Prefer just the color? Turn off Sky details.
  • Break it down. On iPhone models with Apple Intelligence, three tiny first steps for a task that feels too big.
  • Your patterns. After about a week, Wins notices when you get the most done, your strongest day and your longest run, and can offer to move your nudges or evening note to suit you.
  • StandBy. With your iPhone charging on its side, Soonish’s small widget shows your day big.
  • Focus sounds. Brown, pink or white noise under any Focus or Need a minute timer, even with the phone locked.
  • Add from your Mac. See the next question.

Tell us if they’re worth paying for: Settings → Soonish Plus → Worth paying for? Tell us.

How do I add things from my Mac?

With Add from your Mac, a list called Soonish in Apple’s Reminders becomes an inbox. Anything you put there moves into your Scratchpad.

  1. Turn on Reminders in iCloud on your iPhone and your Mac, with the same Apple Account on both.
  2. In Soonish, go to Settings → Soonish Plus → Add from your Mac and allow Full Access to Reminders. Soonish makes the Soonish list for you, in iCloud.
  3. Check the list sits under iCloud in Reminders, not “On My iPhone,” or it won’t reach your Mac.
  4. On your Mac, add to the Soonish list, or say Hey Siri, add call the dentist to my Soonish list. Your Apple Watch and iPad work too.
  5. Open Soonish on your iPhone. Each item lands in your Scratchpad with its day, time, notes and link.

It’s a one-way inbox: once an item is in Soonish, it’s removed from Reminders. Subtasks, images, flags and tags don’t come across, since Apple doesn’t share them with other apps. If something doesn’t show up, give iCloud a minute, then switch away from Soonish and back.

Look and nudges

Why did the sky stop moving?

Your iPhone is probably in Low Power Mode. Soonish holds its skies, water and backgrounds still then, to save battery, and they move again when it’s off. To keep a theme’s colors with nothing moving at all, turn off Sky details in Settings → Theme and app icon.

My widget is dark, but my Home Screen is light.

The widget follows your phone’s light or dark mode, set in Settings → Display & Brightness. If your iPhone is in dark mode but you’ve made just the Home Screen light, the widget stays dark, because apps can’t see that setting.

To fix it, open Soonish and go to Settings → Light or dark, then choose Light. Choose Match phone to go back.

The nudges are too much (or not enough).

Change the times in Settings, or swipe one away to remove it. They always stop as soon as your goal is done today, and never come on days off or while a Focus timer is running.

Your data

Where is my data?

On your iPhone. There’s no account, and nothing is sent to us.

Soonish also makes one backup of its own, once a day: a file in a Soonish folder in your iCloud Drive. If you delete Soonish and install it again, it offers to bring your list back from that file. It holds your tasks, history, badges and settings, but not files or photos attached to tasks, or your theme photo. Turn it off or save a copy anywhere in Settings → Backup and restore.

Your iPhone’s own iCloud Backup is separate. It includes everything in Soonish, attachments too, but it’s only used when you set up a new or erased iPhone, not when you reinstall one app. So keep your own copy of any attachment you can’t lose.

How do I erase everything and start over?

In Settings → Backup and restore, scroll to the bottom and tap Erase everything and start fresh. It clears your list, history, badges, settings, kept thoughts, attached files and theme photo, and Soonish starts like new.

Your backups in iCloud Drive and any copies you saved aren’t touched, so you can bring one back afterward. Attached files and the theme photo aren’t in backups, so those can’t come back.

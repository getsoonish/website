Support

Need a hand? We're happy to help.

Email getsoonishapp@gmail.com, or use Settings → Send feedback in the app. That fills in your app and iOS versions for you.

Testing the beta? Take a screenshot while using Soonish and TestFlight will offer to send it to us with a comment. That's the quickest way to show what you mean.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. If you delete the app, its data is deleted too, so it can't be recovered unless you restore an iCloud or computer backup of your phone.

I ticked something by accident.

On Today, ticked tasks stay on the list for the day. Tap the green circle again to untick it, or tap ••• on the task (or touch and hold it) and choose Mark as not done. You can also find it in the Done tab. It goes back exactly where it was.

I finished something that wasn't in my three. Does it count?

Yes. Everything you finish counts toward your day, Progress and badges, whether it's from the Scratchpad or a bonus round. The ring on Today is your three, and anything extra shows as +1, +2… on it.

I gave a task the wrong date.

Tap ••• on the task and choose Set a date (or Change date). This works on Today too: pick a later day and it leaves today's list and waits for that day.

How do I take a day off?

At the top of Today, tap Take a day off and pick the dates. Days off never break your streak, and the app stays quiet on them. To skip the same days every week, change Active days in Settings.

How do I put a task on a certain day?

Just type the day anywhere in the line: “call mom tomorrow”, “dentist fri”, “rent oct 30” or “10/30”. The task waits in the Scratchpad and joins your three that morning. “this weekend” puts it on Saturday, and “next fri” means Friday of next week. You can also tap ••• on a task and choose Set a date.

What's the difference between a day and a deadline?

A plain date (“fri”) is the day you'll do it, so it stays out of your three until then. Add “by” or “due” (“by fri”) and it's a deadline instead: Soonish may pick it earlier when there's room. You can switch between the two under Set a date or in Edit.

Can tasks repeat?

Yes. Type “every day”, “daily”, “every weekday”, “every mon and thu”, “on tuesdays”, “weekly” or “each day this week”, or tap ••• and choose Repeat to pick the days and an optional last day. Each repeat joins your three on its day. Tick it and the next one waits for its day. Miss a day and it simply moves on: nothing piles up and nothing counts against you.

How does Soonish pick my three?

It looks at due dates, tasks you've marked Important, and how long things have been waiting. Each pick shows a small reason. Don't like one? Swap it out, and use "Back to the suggested three" to undo.

How do badges work?

You earn badges for Steady and Perfect weeks, your best week yet, going above and beyond, doing things you chose yourself, coming back after time away, focus time and milestones. They never expire, and days off never cost you one. Find them on Progress: tap one to bring it to the front, and tap it again to see why you earned it.

What is Need a minute?

For when it's all too much. Tap Need a minute on Today (or use its Lock Screen widget) and everything else hides. Pick one tiny step: a 2-minute reset, a short timer on one task, or Clear my head, where you can write or say what's on your mind. Let it go clears it away and saves nothing; Keep it saves it privately on your phone under Kept thoughts, which open with Face ID.

My widget is dark, but my Home Screen is light.

The Soonish widget follows your phone's light or dark mode, set in Settings → Display & Brightness. iPhone also lets you make just the Home Screen light (touch and hold the Home Screen, then Edit → Customize), but apps can't see that choice, so the widget stays dark. To fix it, open Soonish and go to Settings → Look, then choose Light. The widget will stay light whatever your phone is set to. Choose Match phone to go back.

The nudges are too much (or not enough).

Change the times in Settings, or swipe one away to remove it. They always stop as soon as your three are done, and never come on days off or while a Focus timer is running.

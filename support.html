Where is my data?

On your iPhone. There's no account, and nothing is sent to us.

Soonish also makes one backup of its own, once a day: a file in a Soonish folder in your iCloud Drive (you can see it in the Files app). If you delete Soonish and install it again, it offers to bring your list back from that file. It holds your tasks, history, badges and settings, but not files or photos attached to tasks, or your theme photo. You can turn it off, or save a copy anywhere you like, in Settings → Backup and restore.

That's separate from your iPhone's own iCloud Backup, which Apple makes for the whole phone if you have it on. Apple's backup includes everything in Soonish, attachments too, but it's only used when you set up a new or erased iPhone, not when you reinstall one app. So keep your own copy of any attachment you can't lose.

I checked something off by accident.

On Today, checked-off tasks stay on the list for the day. Tap the green circle again to uncheck it, or tap ••• on the task (or touch and hold it) and choose Mark as not done. You can also find it in the Done tab. It goes back exactly where it was.

I finished something that wasn't in today's picks. Does it count?

Yes. Anything you finish counts toward your daily goal, whether it's one of today's picks or something from the Scratchpad: the ring fills, nudges stop and the day's dot turns green. Everything also counts toward Progress and badges. Anything past your goal shows as +1, +2… on the ring.

I gave a task the wrong date.

Tap ••• on the task and choose Set a date (or Change date). This works on Today too: pick a later day and it leaves today's list and waits for that day.

How do I take a day off?

At the top of Today, tap Take a day off and pick the dates. Days off never break your streak, and the app stays quiet on them. To skip the same days every week, change Active days in Settings.

How do I put a task on a certain day?

Just type the day anywhere in the line: “call mom tomorrow”, “dentist fri”, “rent oct 30” or “10/30”. The task waits in the Scratchpad and joins today's picks that morning. “this weekend” puts it on Saturday, and “next fri” means Friday of next week. You can also tap ••• on a task and choose Set a date.

What's the difference between a day and a deadline?

A plain date (“fri”) is the day you'll do it, so it stays out of today's picks until then. Add “by” or “due” (“by fri”) and it's a deadline instead: Soonish may pick it earlier when there's room. You can switch between the two under Set a date or in Edit.

Can tasks repeat?

Yes. Type “every day”, “daily”, “every weekday”, “every mon and thu”, “on tuesdays”, “weekly” or “each day this week”, or tap ••• and choose Repeat to pick the days and an optional last day. Each repeat joins today's picks on its day. Check it off and the next one waits for its day. Miss a day and it simply moves on: nothing piles up and nothing counts against you.

Can Soonish remind me at a certain time?

Yes. Add a time when you type a task: “call doctor at 3pm”, “dentist fri 9:30am” or just “320p”. You can also tap •••, then Change date and Set time. Then turn on Settings → Heads-up for timed tasks and choose 15 minutes, 30 minutes or an hour before. You get one notification, even on a day off, and none if the task is already done.

How does Soonish pick for me?

Anything due today comes first, then tasks you've marked Important, then what's due soon and how long things have been waiting. A task set for a later day waits for that day, and a deadline more than a week away waits until it's closer. It never gives you more than one task marked Heavy at once (unless two are due today). Each pick shows a small reason. Don't like one? Swap it out, and use "Back to Soonish's picks" to undo.

Done with today and want more? Keep going brings the next thing: anything left for today, then what's coming soonest (tomorrow's first, then the rest of the week), then things with no date. The Scratchpad's Up next always shows the same thing, so they never disagree.

Why only one thing a day?

Starting is the hardest part, so Soonish starts you with one. After a week with something done every day, it asks if you'd like two a day, then three. You can change it any time in Settings → Your daily goal, and doing more is always welcome: it all counts. A day with nothing on your list is a quiet day, and it never breaks your streak.

How do badges work?

You earn badges for Steady and Perfect weeks, your best week yet, going above and beyond, doing things you chose yourself, coming back after time away, focus time and milestones. There are once-a-year ones too, for New Year, St. Patrick's Day, Halloween, Thanksgiving and each new season, and one for your first day off. They never expire, and days off never cost you one. Find them on Progress: tap one to bring it to the front, and tap it again to see why you earned it.

Can I share a badge?

Yes. Tap Share when you earn one, or open any badge on Progress and tap Share. It makes a picture of the badge and why you earned it, never your tasks, and you choose where it goes.

How do I add things from my Mac?

With Add from your Mac (Soonish Plus), a list called Soonish in Apple's Reminders becomes an inbox. Anything you put there moves into your Scratchpad.

  1. Turn on Reminders in iCloud on your iPhone and your Mac (Settings or System Settings → your name → iCloud), with the same Apple Account on both.
  2. In Soonish, go to Settings → Soonish Plus → Add from your Mac and allow Full Access to Reminders. Soonish makes the Soonish list.
  3. Check that the list sits under iCloud in the Reminders app, not “On My iPhone”, or it won't reach your Mac.
  4. On your Mac, add to the Soonish list in Reminders, or say “Hey Siri, add call the dentist to my Soonish list”. Your Apple Watch and iPad work too.
  5. Open Soonish on your iPhone. Each item lands in your Scratchpad, read like anything you type: “pay rent friday” waits for Friday, and a date or time set in Reminders carries over, with any notes and links.

It's a one-way inbox: once an item moves into Soonish, it's checked off in Reminders, so it leaves the Soonish list there (you'll find it under Completed). From then on it lives in Soonish, where you check it off, change its date or edit it. If something doesn't show up, give iCloud a minute, then switch away from Soonish and back. Nothing is ever sent to us; Reminders syncs through your own iCloud.

What is Soonish Plus?

A few extras on top of Soonish, which stays free. They're free for everyone during the beta and marked with a small Plus tag. Turn them on in Settings → Soonish Plus:

  • Today on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. It shows up on its own whenever you open Soonish and today's picks aren't all done, and you can check them off right there. It goes away once your picks are done. The first time, iOS asks to allow Live Activities from Soonish.
  • Focus sounds. Brown, pink or white noise under any Focus or Need a minute timer. It keeps going with the phone locked and stops when the timer ends.
  • Themes and app icons. Calm colors (Clay, Sea glass, Dusk, Rosewood, Brick, Denim), clean ones (Ink, Pearl) and bold ones (Aurora, Holo, your own photo), each with a matching icon and its own color for things you've done. Sunrise, the default, is free.
  • StandBy. With your iPhone charging on its side, Soonish's small widget shows your day big: your picks, or your ring.
  • Your patterns. After about a week, Progress notices when you get the most done, your strongest day, your best day and longest run, and offers to move your nudges or end-of-day note to suit you. A short recap arrives on the 1st of each month, and after about six weeks you can see how your patterns have changed.
  • Add from your Mac. Add to a Soonish list in Apple's Reminders on your Mac, Apple Watch or with Siri, and it moves into your Scratchpad (see above).

Everything is worked out on your phone. Tell us if they're worth paying for: Settings → Soonish Plus → Worth paying for? Tell us.

What is Need a minute?

For when it's all too much. Tap Need a minute on Today (or use its Lock Screen widget) and everything else hides. Pick one tiny step: a 2-minute reset, a short timer on one task, or Clear my head, where you can write or say what's on your mind. Let it go clears it away and saves nothing; Keep it saves it privately on your phone under Kept thoughts, which open with Face ID.

My widget is dark, but my Home Screen is light.

The Soonish widget follows your phone's light or dark mode, set in Settings → Display & Brightness. iPhone also lets you make just the Home Screen light (touch and hold the Home Screen, then Edit → Customize), but apps can't see that choice, so the widget stays dark. To fix it, open Soonish and go to Settings → Look, then choose Light. The widget will stay light whatever your phone is set to. Choose Match phone to go back.

The nudges are too much (or not enough).

Change the times in Settings, or swipe one away to remove it. They always stop as soon as your goal is done today, and never come on days off or while a Focus timer is running.

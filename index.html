Why I made Soonish

I have ADHD, and to-do apps never stuck for me. Seeing everything I needed to do at once made me freeze. The red badge number kept climbing until I stopped looking, and eventually I'd delete the app and feel disappointed in myself for not sticking with something that was meant to help.

So I built the opposite: something that would help me actually get through my list. You put everything into the Scratchpad, and each day it shows you just three things. It nudges you gently, then goes quiet once they're done, and a bad day or a day off never counts against you. Instead of a red number on the icon, a widget on your Home Screen shows your three, and you can tick them off right there. You earn badges for what you get done, and you're never shamed for what you don't.

I made it for people like me, but a lot of people get overwhelmed by how most to-do apps work and just need to take it one day, one task, at a time. I hope it helps you the way it's helped me.

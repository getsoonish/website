Why I made Soonish

“Seeing everything I needed to do at once made me freeze.”

I have ADHD, and to-do apps never stuck for me. Seeing everything I needed to do at once made me freeze. The red badge number kept climbing until I stopped looking, and eventually I’d delete the app and feel disappointed in myself for not sticking with something that was meant to help.

I tried all the big to-do apps, and they looked great, but I spent more time setting up and sorting my lists than actually doing anything on them. It just took way too much time to set up, more than my brain felt it was worth.

So I built the opposite. The first version picked three things a day. Then I lived with it, and on hard days even three was too much. I caught myself marking days off that weren’t really days off, just so my streak wouldn’t break. So now Soonish starts with one.

Read the rest

You drop everything into the Scratchpad, and each day it shows you one thing. When that starts to feel easy, it asks if you’d like two, then three, but it never asks for more than three, and you can always go back.

Soonish nudges you gently, then goes quiet once you’re done. On a day off, or a day with nothing on your list, it doesn’t nudge you at all. Instead of a red number on the icon, a widget on your Home Screen shows what’s next. You earn badges for what you get done, and you’re never shamed for what you don’t.

I made it for people like me, but a lot of people get overwhelmed by how most to-do apps work and just need to take it one day, one task, at a time. I hope it helps you the way it’s helped me.

Soonish does less, on purpose.

  • Priority levels on every task
    Mark what matters Important, if you like. Soonish sorts the rest.
  • Tags you made once and forgot
    Search finds it for you.
  • An “overdue” label
    What you didn’t get to comes back tomorrow, with no label.
  • A streak that breaks after one bad day
    Rough day? Count it as a day off the next morning. Your week stays whole.
  • A red number that keeps climbing
    No number unless you turn it on, and then only what’s left of today’s picks. It only counts down.
